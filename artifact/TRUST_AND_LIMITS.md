# What this offline audit establishes

This artifact exposes the chain from a declared feasible witness through the
whole returned representation and recorded processing events. In the separate
legacy checker fixture, it also exposes fixed-input verdict changes. It is a
retrospective evidence audit, not a general semantic verifier.

## Independent references and scope

The old fixture's reference enumerates bounded-action trajectories with exact
rational arithmetic and its declared observation operator. It is a finite
underapproximation of feasible states, sufficient for its exhibited omission,
not an exhaustive continuous reachable set. Its final 293 hypotheses are one
epoch's finite reference set.

The sensor fixture's stationary body certificate is derived from fixed ideal
panel geometry and camera/body ray intersections. Depth observations, camera
poses and native outputs are archived. Sensor feasibility and the old fixture's
observation-equivalence argument are distinct pieces of evidence.

The output decoder checks position projection for supported serialized tree
nodes. Presence alone does not certify velocity/history coverage. Unknown lanes,
unbounded terminal nodes and truncated leaf depths remain explicit unresolved
states. A separate velocity-envelope discrepancy is not attributed to C1.

## Mechanism and interventions

The C1 reduction substitutes a same-update processing record for the live
coverage needed after later work in that update. C1 does not establish a
cross-epoch stale-cache bug. An earlier mature output can exist and still fail
to preserve the later required state. `mature=None` is therefore not a universal
precondition.

Local single admission and full-sequence admission disablement have different
causal scopes. The former uses the recorded identical prefix and target input.
The latter can change prior history. Do not relabel every later difference as a
same-prefix effect. Likewise, a rejected candidate need not cause harmful
omission if an alternate output retains the witness.

Simple conservative fixes restore the designated C1 omissions in the inspected
small workloads. This does not demonstrate a need for a new complex algorithm
or an unavoidable safety/efficiency tradeoff. Native generation and constructed
boundary fixtures remain separately identified.

## Consumption and physical claims

The old fixed checker fixture and synthetic-depth scene are not one combined
end-to-end experiment. Fourteen changed permission verdicts among eighteen
candidate tuples are not fourteen collisions. A permission difference is distinct
from an independent safety-criterion violation, model-level inevitability,
physical contact, task completion, and closed-loop robot safety.

The archived no-effect/negative records remain in the archive. A target that did
not occur, intervention that did not apply, decoder that does not support an
input, and successful preservation are not equivalent negative results.

## Provenance and executable scope

The public archive's 647 members have independent SHA256 checks. Personal paths
and private links were scrubbed; source references were rehashed transparently.
Normalized scientific output records remain equal to the baseline except for
the six explicitly corrected cause labels. All binary NPZ payloads remain
unchanged. This demonstrates integrity of this public transformation, not the
physical truth of a simulation model.

The validator never imports native planner modules or unpickles contexts.
Four historical replay-only pickle inputs are not contained in this offline
artifact. Their recorded collection-time hashes are documentation, not a claim
that this command reproduces native checker execution. Historical source and
lock files in the ZIP aid inspection; they do not provide all native assets.

No real RFM, physical robot, CARLA closed-loop scene, or completion experiment
is newly executed by this audit. The standard-library consistency-check total
must not be used as an empirical sample size or detector accuracy score.
